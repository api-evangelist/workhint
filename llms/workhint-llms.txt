# Workhint

> Workhint is a work orchestration platform that uses AI to configure workflows, roles, and operations from modular components, helping teams manage flexible, on-demand workforces — sourcing, onboarding, tasks, shifts, field visits, invoicing, global payments, payouts, contracts, and 1099 compliance. Backed by 500 Global and Softeq. API access and custom integrations are offered on the Enterprise plan; no public developer documentation is published.
